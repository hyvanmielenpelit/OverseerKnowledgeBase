---
title: Vault Features
summary: Library, Oracle consultations, Snapshots, and Soundtracks
---

#### 1. Library
- View discovered in-game manuals and lore books.
- Also accessible directly from the in-game menu while playing.

#### 2. Delphi
- View unlocked prophecies obtained from visiting the Oracle in the dungeon.
- Accessible via the Vault menu or from the in-game menu (labeled "Delphi").

#### 3. Snapshots
- View saved gameplay screenshots.
- Screenshots are captured by selecting **Take Snapshot** from the in-game menu.

#### 4. Soundtracks
- A jukebox feature for playback of the game's music tracks.
