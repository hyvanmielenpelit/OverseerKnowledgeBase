---
title: Achievements & Tier Ranks
summary: Achievement system, tier rank progression, and tracking
---

#### 1. How Achievements Work
Achievements are unlocked by reaching specific gameplay milestones (e.g., reaching a new dungeon level, defeating a boss, or completing a unique action).

#### 2. Viewing Achievements
- **Vault → Achievements**
- You can view cards for each achievement to see your progress or requirements.

#### 3. Tier Ranks
- Completing achievements earns progress towards Tier Ranks.
- Higher ranks require completing more challenging milestones.

#### 4. Completion Tracking
- Your overall achievement completion percentage is tracked and displayed in the Vault.

#### 5. Notifications
- When you unlock enough achievements to reach a new tier, you will receive a "New Rank Claimed" notification in-game.
