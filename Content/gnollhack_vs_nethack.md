#### 1. Overview of Key Differences
GnollHack is based on NetHack 3.6.2 but features significant changes including a modern graphical UI with animations, a completely overhauled combat system with new ranged weapons, an expanded magic system with spell schools, new item quality tiers (mythic, legendary), runewords, 7 distinct difficulty levels, and four gameplay modes.

#### 2. Quick Answer Reference
- **Magic system:** significantly expanded (new spell schools, mana system)
- **Combat:** new ranged system, weapon skills reworked
- **Items:** new quality tiers (exceptional, elite, celestial, etc.), runewords, mythic/legendary
- **Difficulty:** 7 difficulty levels (NetHack had none)
- **Game modes:** Classic, Modern, Casual, Tournament
- **Scoring:** completely reworked with conduct bonuses and difficulty multipliers
